# Smart Retail AI — SAP Hackfest MVP

## Core Goal
Help small retailers make smarter inventory and purchasing decisions using data and AI.

## Must-Build Features

### 1. Inventory Management
Status: Working
- Add products
- Track current stock
- Set minimum stock levels
- Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts

### 2. Demand Prediction
Status: In Progress
- Predict future product demand based on historical data

### 3. Price Prediction
Status: In Progress
- Identify possible product price increases

### 4. Supplier Comparison
Status: In Progress
- Compare supplier prices
- Compare delivery time
- Recommend the best supplier

### 5. Language Support
Status: In Progress
- Support multiple languages for easier use

## Integration Features

### 6. Smart Buying Recommendation
Combine inventory, demand, price, and supplier data to recommend:
- What to buy
- How much to buy
- When to buy
- Which supplier to choose

### 7. Bulk and Repack Management
For products such as sugar, rice, and dal:
- Record bulk stock received
- Convert bulk stock into smaller packs
- Automatically update remaining bulk stock
- Track created saleable packs
